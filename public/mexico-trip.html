Family trip · Aug 3–8, 2026

Mexico City

Based at the Marriott Reforma · 6 days · Zona Rosa, Chapultepec, Centro, Coyoacán & a big day trip

Before you go each day

  • Book ahead. Frida Kahlo Museum (La Casa Azul) tickets sell out — get them online in advance. For Teotihuacán, an organized half-day tour with pickup is easier than driving yourself.
  • No Mondays here. Tue–Sun works well for this trip — several museums (Chapultepec Castle, Museo Nacional de Antropología, the Zoo, Templo Mayor) are closed on Mondays, and you never hit one.
  • Getting around. Reforma and Zona Rosa are very walkable from the hotel. Everything else — Chapultepec, Centro, Coyoacán, Teotihuacán — is a quick, inexpensive Uber.

Packing list

CDMX sits at ~7,350 ft — mild fatigue or shortness of breath on day one is normal, so keep water handy. August is rainy season: mornings and evenings are usually clear, with brief afternoon showers.
